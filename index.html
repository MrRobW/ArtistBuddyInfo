Privacy Policy for ArtistBuddy

Last Updated: May 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how ArtistBuddy ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your artistic data remains under your exclusive control.

Core Philosophy: ArtistBuddy is a privacy-first tool. It follows an on-device processing model. All image analysis, color matching, and project management occur locally on your smartphone. We do not operate external servers to store or process your creative work.

1. Information Collection and Use

Camera Access

ArtistBuddy requires access to your device’s camera to facilitate its live overlay and projection features.

  • Real-time Processing: The camera feed is used strictly for real-time rendering on your device's screen to assist with sketching and color sampling.
  • No Recording/Storage: The camera stream is never recorded, stored, or transmitted by the App.

Files and Media

To allow you to analyze your own photos or save progress, the App requires permission to access your device’s storage.

  • Local Storage: Imported images, pigment inventory data, and saved projects are stored locally in the App's private directory.
  • No Uploads: Your images and project files are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third parties.

2. Data Safety and Security

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect personal identifiers, usage statistics, or behavioral data.
  • No Tracking: ArtistBuddy does not contain advertisements, hidden trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • On-Device Integrity: Your data never leaves your device; security is managed by your own device's encryption and sandboxing.

3. Children’s Privacy

ArtistBuddy does not knowingly collect any personal information. It is safe for use by artists of all ages, as no data is transmitted, shared, or requested.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses standard Android system libraries (such as CameraX and Room Database) provided by Google. These libraries operate within the App’s secure sandbox and do not facilitate external data sharing.
