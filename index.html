Privacy Policy for Artist Buddy

Last Updated: October 2023

Artist Buddy is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. On-Device Processing

Artist Buddy is designed as an "on-device" utility. We believe that your creative process and data should remain private. All heavy lifting, including image processing and color analysis, happens locally on your smartphone.

2. Camera Access

The app requests camera permission to provide the live overlay/projection feature. The camera feed is used strictly for real-time rendering on your screen to assist with sketching. This feed is never recorded, stored, or transmitted by the app.

3. Images and Data Storage

Images you import and project data you create (such as pigment inventory and saved projects) are stored locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, or share your images or project data with any external servers.

4. No Third-Party Tracking

Artist Buddy does not contain advertisements or tracking SDKs that profile your behavior. We do not share your data with any third-party services.
